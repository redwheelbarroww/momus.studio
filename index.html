What synthetic audience means

Structured scenario research, not one generic AI opinion.

Momus runs the same decision through multiple defined buyer postures, then codes recurring objections, trust gaps, comparisons, and unclear claims. It is fast directional evidence for deciding what to test next—not a replacement for analytics or real-user validation.

01

Map the decision

Define the audience, asset or idea, desired action, alternatives, and constraints.

02

Run the audience

Simulate urgent, skeptical, careful, budget-sensitive, and overwhelmed audience postures.

03

Code friction

Tag objections, proof gaps, message mismatch, unclear claims, and alternative pull.

04

Prioritize changes

Deliver the fixes, tests, and questions most likely to improve the decision.
